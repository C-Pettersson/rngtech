# Modular Field Tools

Status: Prototype

Modular Field Tools are player-held work tools assembled from a Tool Head, Tool Rod, and optional Battery Cell. The head and rod each define authored base stats, rarity, affixes, and Refinement Potential. Assembled tools are blank carriers: they store installed part stacks, damage, and optional Battery Cell data, then derive effective stats from the current installed parts.

## Implemented Content

| Content | Resource ids | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tool Bench | `rngtech:tool_bench` | Simple placed station with Build, Gear, Stats, and Refine tabs. The Build tab has tool, head, rod, Battery Cell, and repair material slots; it assembles new tools, swaps installed parts, repairs damaged tools, disassembles assembled tools back into their stored parts, validates Battery Support, and returns removed components with stored data intact. The Gear tab has a reusable Tiny Anvil slot that unlocks Hammer, Digger, and Treefeller head assembly, plus one Energy Connector slot that enables block FE input for charging the inserted modular tool's installed Battery Cell or a Miner's Companion in the Tool slot. The Stats tab shows the inserted modular tool's effective stats with hover explanations. The Refine tab has Tool, Head, Rod, and catalyst entry slots and applies Affix Injector, Ascension Catalyst, and Ascension Matrix to the inserted tool's installed Tool Head or Tool Rod, or to loose Tool Head and Tool Rod stacks when no valid tool is inserted. |
| Tiny Anvil | `rngtech:tiny_anvil` | Reusable Tool Bench Gear item. It is required when assembling a new Hammer, Digger, or Treefeller, or when swapping a tool head into one of those bulk families. |
| Diamond Tip | `rngtech:diamond_tip` | Consumable Tool Bench upgrade for untipped Steel Pick Heads and Steel Hammer Heads. The upgrade is stored on the head stack and raises ore-hardness reach by `+1`. |
| Assembled tools | `rngtech:modular_pick`, `rngtech:modular_hammer`, `rngtech:modular_shovel`, `rngtech:modular_digger`, `rngtech:modular_axe`, `rngtech:modular_treefeller` | Dedicated item ids per action family. ## Runtime Behavior

Picks, Shovels, and Axes break one matching block. Hammers break a `3x3` area around the target face when extra targets are valid pick-minable blocks. Diggers use the same area behavior for shovel-minable blocks such as dirt, gravel, sand, clay, and snow. Treefellers break connected logs up to the effective `TREE_FELL_LIMIT`. Picks with the rare Vein Miner Pick Head prefix break connected same-type ore blocks up to `VEIN_MINE_LIMIT` unless the player is sneaking. Assembled tools expose a main-hand attack-speed attribute derived from the installed Tool Head family plus the installed Tool Rod's swing adjustment.

Hammer, Digger, and Treefeller assembly requires a Tiny Anvil installed in the Tool Bench Gear tab. The Tiny Anvil is not consumed and normal Picks, Shovels, and Axes remain available from a fresh Tool Bench.

Durability and FE are charged per block that is actually broken. Vein Miner also requires `VEIN_MINE_FE_USAGE` extra FE for each connected block after the targeted block; when that surcharge cannot be paid, remaining connected blocks are skipped. Stability above `1.0` gives a per-block chance to skip both durability and FE cost equal to the amount above `1.0`; for example, `1.10` Stability gives a `10%` cost-skip chance. If Stability does not skip the cost, Control above `1.0` deterministically moves part of the durability cost onto the installed Battery Cell as family-based shield FE. The Control FE share is `control - 1.0`, capped at `100%`; for example, `1.60` Control pays `60%` of each durability point as FE and leaves `40%` as accumulated fractional durability wear. Full shield FE costs are Pick `16 FE`, Shovel `12 FE`, Axe `16 FE`, Hammer `32 FE`, Digger `28 FE`, and Treefeller `28 FE`; the current Control share scales that cost and rounds it to whole FE. If full shield FE cannot be paid from an accepted installed Battery Cell, tools continue with durability-only work for that block. When durability reaches `0`, the tool remains in the stack but stops acting as a modular tool until repaired. Installed Battery Cells expose an item energy capability through the tool stack, and compatible inventory Battery Cells can recharge the installed cell in deterministic inventory order when their stage is within the tool's effective Battery Support. A Tool Bench with an installed Energy Connector exposes FE input and forwards received FE into the inserted modular tool, capped by the connector tier and the tool's effective FE transfer. The same Tool slot charging path accepts Miner's Companions and charges their installed Battery Cells through the Miner's Companion item energy capability.

Tool Heads and Tool Rods can roll a Regenerative Self Repair prefix. The assembled tool adds the installed parts' `SELF_REPAIR` values and restores that much durability every `7.5` seconds while it is idle in the player's hotbar or offhand. Active mining or tool-use pauses the repair. Tier 2 restores `4` durability per interval, while tiers 3 and 4 restore `6` and `8`.

Damaged or broken modular tools can be repaired in the Tool Bench repair material slot or in an anvil with the matching head material. Metal heads use the matching ingot, while Flint heads use flint. The Tool Bench consumes one matching material per `Apply` action and restores up to one quarter of the tool's maximum durability. Its Disassemble action removes the carrier tool and returns the stored head, rod, and optional Battery Cell. Generic two-tool crafting repair is intentionally disabled for modular tools so the installed component data is not discarded.

The same Tool Bench repair material slot accepts a Diamond Tip as a head upgrade. It can be applied to a loose Steel Pick Head or Steel Hammer Head, during new tool assembly, or to the installed/replacement head of an inserted modular tool. The tip is consumed, stored on the Tool Head stack, and raises that head's effective `MINING_LEVEL` from `3` to `4`, which opens the Aluminum/Osmium ore band before an Aluminum head is craftable.

Ore Burst applies only to Pick and Hammer tools with ore-burst-capable material identity. It increases break speed only on blocks tagged `rngtech:ore_burst_targets` and charges extra FE only for ore blocks that are actually broken.

RNGTech ores add an ore-hardness gate separate from block destroy speed, vanilla tier tags, component stage, and Crusher `required_processing_level`. Blocks tagged `rngtech:ore_hardness/level_<n>` require a Modular Pick or Hammer with effective `MINING_LEVEL >= n`, except for the configured vanilla bridge. The default bridge lets stone-or-better vanilla pickaxes harvest hardness `1` and iron-or-better vanilla pickaxes harvest hardness `2`; hardness `3+` requires modular tool reach unless the pack changes `worldgen.ores.vanillaToolBridgePolicy`. Flint and Iron heads reach `1`, Copper and Bronze heads reach `2`, Steel heads reach `3`, Diamond-tipped Steel Pick/Hammer Heads reach `4`, Aluminum heads reach `5`, Titanium heads reach `6`, Tungstensteel and Nullite heads reach `7`, and Exotic heads reach `8`. Hammers evaluate this per target and skip over-hard ore blocks without breaking them or charging durability/FE for those skipped blocks.

Luck is tool-local. It can add modest extra drops only for modular-tool block drops from ore-burst targets and logs; for RNGTech ores this happens after the ore-hardness gate passes and the ore loot table has produced its normal raw material drops. It does not affect machines, recipes, chests, mobs, or unrelated loot tables.

## Refinement

Tool Heads and Tool Rods are normal Affix Forge targets. The Tool Bench Refine tab can also refine the installed head or rod inside an assembled tool. It uses the Affix Forge-style outcome preview without upgrade slots. It exposes the Tool slot for installed-part refinement and loose Head and Rod slots for fallback targets when no valid tool is inserted. It accepts Affix Injector, Ascension Catalyst, and Ascension Matrix. Lenses, removals, rerolls, Expansion Crystal, and modifier crystals belong to the Affix Forge until the Tool Bench gets its own focus-slot pass.

Assembled modular tools are not refinement or debug-reroll targets, and they do not roll or keep their own affixes. Swapping parts returns removed components with their stored rolls intact, while the assembled tool immediately reflects the base stats and affixes of the currently installed head and rod.

Tool Head and Tool Rod rolls use field-tool tuning: their Refinement Potential range is the normal component-stage range plus `2`, and their affix tier rolls have a small high-tier bias compared with generic components. Pick Heads use a pick-specific profile that can roll the rare Vein Miner prefix; non-pick heads continue to use the general Tool Head profile.

## Current Limits

The first slice uses simple shaped recipes for heads and rods. Resonance Calibrator rod stabilization gates are still a design target until calibrated tool-part recipes are added. There is no mode UI for Hammer or Digger size or target filtering yet; the implemented area behavior uses the authored area stats directly.
