# Identifying Fluid

The Identifier uses Identifying Fluid. Make it in a [Melter](rngtech:melter.md) from Naquadah Dust, a Calibrated Diamond Crystal, and Ender Slurry. The recipe needs an Exotic Heat Core.

| Input | Output | Time | Heat | Min. temperature | Processing level | Bonus output | Mastery XP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:naquadah_dust" /> *(Primary ingredient)*<br />**Calibrated Diamond Crystal** *(Secondary ingredient)*<br />Ender Slurry (1,000 mB) *(Fluid input)* | Identifying Fluid (1,000 mB) *(Fluid output)* | 20 s | 6,000 HU | 1,500 | 8 | No | 8 |

To fill the tank, pipe the fluid in, or put a bucket of Identifying Fluid in the bucket slot. The tank holds 8,000 mB before rolls.
