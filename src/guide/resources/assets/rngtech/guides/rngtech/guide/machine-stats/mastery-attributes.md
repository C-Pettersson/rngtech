# Mastery attributes

**Control**, **Drive**, and **Reserve** are Machine Mastery attributes: precision, actuation, and capacity. Points come from the Mastery tree and from [attribute suffixes](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes/attribute-suffixes.md) on the machine and its Gear. Each machine family turns its points into its own stats, such as speed or buffer size. See [Machine Mastery](rngtech:machine-mastery.md). On a Tool Rod, Control also spends FE to protect durability.
