# Getting the gases

- **Nitrogen**: separate it from water in a Stage 6 [Cavitation Generator](rngtech:cavitation-generator.md) fitted with a Nitrogen Extraction Rotor and a Nitrogen Separation Nozzle.
- **Hydrogen**: from the [Steam Methane Reformer](rngtech:steam-methane-reformer.md).
- **Carbon Exhaust**, for Urea: from a [Syngas Combustor](rngtech:syngas-combustor.md), a [Wooden Dehumidifier](rngtech:wooden-dehumidifier.md), a Steam Methane Reformer running the [water-gas shift](rngtech:steam-methane-reformer/water-gas-shift.md), or a [Bio Generator](rngtech:bio-generator/methane-take-off.md) with a Biogas Membrane.
