# Finding Uniques

Each Unique comes from one kind of place.

| Unique | Gear | Counts as | Found in |
|---|---|---:|---|
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:unique_potato_battery_cell" /> | Battery Cell | Stage 0 | Village chests, 5% per chest |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:igloo_basement_thermostat" /> | Heat Core | Stage 2 | Igloo basement chests, 10% |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:mineshaft_worn_pick_jaw" /> | Crush Head | Stage 2 | Mineshaft minecart chests, 4% |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:crying_crucible" /> | Alloy Crucible | Stage 3 | Ruined portal chests, 4% |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:fortress_heater_element" /> | Heat Core | Stage 4 | Nether fortress chests, 8% |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:bastion_coin_stack_capacitor" /> | Battery Cell | Stage 4 | Bastion treasure chests, 10% |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:witch_bottle_reflux_pump" /> | Fluid Pump | Stage 5 | Witches killed by a player, 1%, plus 0.5% per Looting level |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:trial_vault_escapement" /> | Servo | Stage 6 | Ominous trial vaults, 3% per vault |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:ancient_echo_control_board" /> | Control Board | Stage 6 | Ancient city chests, 4% |

A [Work Order](rngtech:work-orders/rewards.md) with Prestige 2 or more can also pay an unidentified Unique. It is most often one that fits the job's machine.

Your modpack may move Uniques to other sources, such as quest rewards.
