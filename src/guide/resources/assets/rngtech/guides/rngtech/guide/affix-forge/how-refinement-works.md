# How refinement works

Every refinable item has a [rarity](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md) and a budget of [Refinement Potential](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md#refinement-potential) (RP):

- **Rarity** sets how many affixes the item can hold. Normal items have none, Magic items hold one prefix and one suffix, and Rare items hold up to three of each.
- **RP** is spent by every successful operation. When an item runs out, you cannot refine it further, so plan where you spend it.

A failed or illegal operation costs nothing: no catalyst and no RP. Identify an item before you refine it (see [Getting Started](rngtech:getting-started.md)). Unidentified items, recycled (stripped) items, and [Corrupted](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md#corruption) items cannot be refined. Unique items accept only a Volatile Catalyst.

Affix and tier odds are weighted, not guaranteed. Tier 7 affixes can appear only on Stage 8 Exotic items, and only when an affix is added or rerolled. Upgrades and the Ascension catalysts stop at Tier 6. See [Rarity and Affixes](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes/affix-tiers.md) for tiers and weights.
