# Glyphs

<ItemLink id="rngtech:glyph" /> have no recipe. They come in tiers I to V and are earned by running RNGTech machines hard:

| Source | Glyph |
|---|---|
| A machine reaching Mastery level 20 or 40 | Tier I |
| A machine reaching Mastery level 60 or 80 | Tier II |
| A machine reaching Mastery level 100 | Tier III |
| A Stage 6 or higher [calibration](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) at 90% stability or more | 15% chance of Tier II; Stage 8 at 95% gives Tier III |
| A [Forestry Companion](rngtech:forestry-cart-station.md) harvest with a Stage 7 or higher head | 2% chance of Tier II |
| A [Vacuum Collapse](rngtech:vacuum-collapse-generator.md) cycle at instability 60 or more | 10% chance of Tier III; 90 or more gives Tier IV |
| A [Crusher](rngtech:crusher.md) jam on a Processing Level 7 or higher recipe | 5% chance of Tier I |
| Waking a [Dormant Machine](rngtech:dormant-machines.md) | Tier I to IV by site |

Tier V has no source yet. A Glyph with no tier counts as Tier I, so one from a quest or a command works.

A Glyph has two uses, and they compete: **lock** it into a Coil to open that position for good, or **spend** it on a deep operation. The tier is a floor for both: a Tier III Glyph opens any position up to 7 and pays for any operation that asks for Tier III or less.
