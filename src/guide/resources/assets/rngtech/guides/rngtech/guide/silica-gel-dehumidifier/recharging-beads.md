# Recharging beads

Smelt saturated beads in an RNGTech [Furnace](rngtech:furnace.md) to dry them out again. They dry at 120 °C, like real silica gel. Recharging gives back exactly the bead you put in, never a bonus bead. Beads pushed past their overheat temperature with the [overheat slider](rngtech:heat/the-overheat-slider.md) can burn to **Ash**, which you can put in a Composter.
