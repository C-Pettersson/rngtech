# Ores

RNGTech adds eleven ores to the Overworld, each in a stone and a deepslate version. Iron, Copper, and Gold use the vanilla ores. Each ore has a **hardness** that your pick or hammer head must reach; see [Ore hardness](rngtech:modular-tools.md#ore-hardness) for which heads reach which level.

The deeper ores are often cut out of cave walls, so dig into solid rock instead of only searching caves. With the JEI mod installed, each ore has an ore generation page showing its depth, vein size, and hardness for your pack's settings.

Mining an RNGTech ore drops its raw metal, such as <ItemLink id="rngtech:raw_tin" />. Fortune increases the drop like it does on vanilla ores, and Silk Touch drops the ore block itself.
