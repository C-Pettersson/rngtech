# Where the products go

- **Hydrogen** feeds the [Ammonia Synthesizer](rngtech:ammonia-synthesizer.md), which makes Ammonia for the [Fuel Cell](rngtech:ammonia-fuel-cell.md). The Fuel Cell can also [burn Hydrogen directly](rngtech:ammonia-fuel-cell/ammonia-or-hydrogen.md), for less FE.
- **Carbon Monoxide** is the best carbon input for the [Algae Photobioreactor](rngtech:algae-photobioreactor.md). A second Reformer can [shift](rngtech:steam-methane-reformer/water-gas-shift.md) it into more Hydrogen, and the [Syngas Combustor](rngtech:syngas-combustor.md) can burn it as a weaker fuel to get rid of it.
- **Carbon Exhaust** from a shift is a slower carbon input for the Algae Photobioreactor. The [Ammonia Synthesizer](rngtech:ammonia-synthesizer.md) also takes it to make Urea.
