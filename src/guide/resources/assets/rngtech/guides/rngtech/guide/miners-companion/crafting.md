# Crafting

<RecipesFor id="rngtech:miners_companion" />

- Requires the Iron material to be enabled.
- Requires the Copper material to be enabled.

The crafted Miner's Companion comes out Unidentified, like other trait items, and rolls as a Stage 2 item. It does not stack. See [Rarity and Affixes](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md) for how to identify it.
