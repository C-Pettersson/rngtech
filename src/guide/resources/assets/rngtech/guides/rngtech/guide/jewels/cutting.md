# Cutting

A Stage 3 or higher [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) cuts a Jewel Blank into a jewel. Use the Structural pattern. The catalyst picks the colour:

| Catalyst | Jewel |
|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:prismarine_crystals" /> | <ItemLink id="rngtech:control_jewel" /> |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:gold_ingot" /> | <ItemLink id="rngtech:drive_jewel" /> |
| <ItemLink id="minecraft:redstone_block" /> | <ItemLink id="rngtech:reserve_jewel" /> |

The cut's stability sets the jewel's Refinement Potential: 2 RP below 50%, 3 RP from 50%, and 4 RP from 80%. A jewel comes out of the Calibrator **Unidentified**. Put it alone in a crafting grid to identify it, like any other [rolled item](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes/identification.md).
