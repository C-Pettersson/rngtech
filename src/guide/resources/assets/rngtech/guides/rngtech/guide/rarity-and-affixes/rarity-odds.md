# Rarity odds

The odds depend on the item's [stage](rngtech:stages.md):

| Item stage | Normal | Magic | Rare |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 35% | 50% | 15% |
| 1–4 | — | 70% | 30% |
| 5 and higher | 50% | 40% | 10% |

Stage 1–4 items are never Normal, so the gear you build early always shows some affixes. From Stage 5 up, a Normal roll is common, and the [Affix Forge](rngtech:affix-forge.md) does more of the work.

The <ItemLink id="rngtech:miners_companion" /> and the Forestry Companion roll as Stage 2 items. The Crude and Steel [Metal Press](rngtech:metal-press.md) roll as Stage 8 items, with Stage 8 tiers and RP, because they have no upgrade ladder to carry your investment forward.
