# Heat

The [Furnace](rngtech:furnace.md), [Alloy Furnace](rngtech:alloy-furnace.md), [Metal Press](rngtech:metal-press.md), and [Melter](rngtech:melter.md) all heat their work the same way. Each lane has a live temperature. A heater pushes heat in, the walls leak it out, and the input soaks it up as it cooks. You pay FE, or fuel on the Stage 0 Furnace, for the heat the heater puts in, including the heat the walls lose.
