# Using a Unique

- A Unique fits the same Gear slots as a normal part of its type, and follows the same stage rules using the stage it counts as. The <ItemLink id="rngtech:mineshaft_worn_pick_jaw" /> counts as Stage 2, so a Copper Crusher accepts it, even though it crushes at hardness 3.
- A machine can hold a Unique in every slot that accepts one.
- Inside a machine's screen, a Unique's tooltip greys out any stat or behavior that machine does not use, marked "not used here".
- An ascendancy bonus does nothing until the machine has chosen that ascendancy; you don't need any of its nodes. The tooltip says which ascendancy it needs, and inside a machine that hasn't chosen it the line is greyed out. It adds to the same stat the ascendancy's own nodes give.
- Stats a Unique gives a machine, such as Blend Speed or Jam Chance per Cycle, appear on that machine's Stats tab while the Unique is installed. See [Machine Stats](rngtech:machine-stats/unique-stats.md).
- Uniques keep their rolls when you break the machine they are in.

Uniques have no Refinement Potential and cannot be refined: the [Affix Forge](rngtech:affix-forge.md) and [Phase Array](rngtech:phase-array.md) refuse every catalyst except the <ItemLink id="rngtech:volatile_catalyst" />. A <ItemLink id="rngtech:mirror_catalyst" /> cannot copy a Unique. [Corruption](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md#corruption) is the only way to change a Unique, once: Reforged rerolls every ranged stat line inside its range, Blessed and Blighted add an implicit from the Unique's part, and Warped leaves it unchanged. The [Component Recycler](rngtech:component-recycler.md) and [Potential Reactor](rngtech:potential-reactor.md) will not take them either.
