# Calibration and refinement

| Stat | What it does | Used by | Better |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refinement Potential | The item's remaining budget for refinement. See [Refinement Potential](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md#refinement-potential). | Everything that rolls traits. | Higher |
| Calibration Quality | Raises the stability floor and ceiling of calibrated outputs. | [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) | Higher |
| Calibration Precision | Narrows the stability range of calibrated outputs. | [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) | Higher |
| Catalyst Efficiency | Above 1.0, a chance to keep the catalyst after a calibration, up to 45%. | [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) | Higher |
| Output RP Bonus | Adds Refinement Potential to calibrated outputs. | [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) | Higher |
