# Diamond Tip

A <ItemLink id="rngtech:diamond_tip" /> in the repair slot upgrades a Steel Pick Head or Steel Hammer Head, loose or installed, from ore-hardness 3 to 4. This lets you mine Aluminum and Osmium ores before you can craft an Aluminum head. The tip is used up.

<RecipesFor id="rngtech:diamond_tip" />

- Requires the Steel material to be enabled.
