# Failures from Stage 4

Stages 0–3 are forgiving. When conditions are wrong, early machines wait, slow down, or produce less, and they keep your input.

From Stage 4, some recipes can fail. Heat outside the safe window, poor temperature stability, or a power drop in the middle of a job builds up failure strain, and when it fills, the input becomes a recoverable failure item such as a **Malformed Ingot** or **Broken Circuit**. Smelt a Malformed Ingot in a [Furnace](rngtech:furnace.md) to get two nuggets back. Better Heat Cores, Servos, and Battery Cells make the same work safer. See the [Metal Press](rngtech:metal-press/pressing.md) and [Furnace](rngtech:furnace/smelting.md) for how each machine handles it.
