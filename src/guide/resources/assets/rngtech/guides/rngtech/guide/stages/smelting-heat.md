# Smelting heat

Every form of a metal needs the same smelting heat: raw metal, ore blocks, crushed material, and dust. Your Furnace's Heat Core decides which metals you can smelt at all, whatever form they are in. Raw metal smelts like its ore block, so smelting it directly is the slow, heat-hungry way to get one ingot; crushing it gives two, and dust is the cheapest to smelt.

| Metal | Minimum heat | Needs stability |
|---|---:|---|
| Iron, Copper | 800 | No |
| Tin, Zinc, Gold | 1,000 | No |
| Nickel, Lead, Silver | 1,100 | Yes |
| Aluminum, Osmium | 1,300 | Yes |
| Titanium | 1,450 | Yes |
| Tungsten, Platinum | 1,750 | Yes |
| Naquadah | 2,000 | Yes |

See the [Furnace](rngtech:furnace/smelting.md) for the heat each Heat Core reaches.

Higher-stage metals also cost more FE. Smelting and crushing Stage 5, 6, 7, and 8 metals cost 2×, 3×, 4×, and 6× the Stage 1–4 FE, and their crushing recipes need harder [Crush Heads](rngtech:crusher/hardness.md).
