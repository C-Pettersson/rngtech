# Annealed items

An item that rolls traits comes out **Annealed** when you craft it at a crafting table and every plate in its recipe is Annealed. Annealed plates come from a [Metal Press with Hydrogen Quench](rngtech:metal-press/annealing.md). Only the plates in the item's own crafting grid count, so plates used earlier, for example in a Machine Frame, do not. Items whose recipe has no plates, such as Heat Cores, cannot be Annealed.

The Unidentified item shows an aqua Annealed line. When you identify or place it, its RP rolls twice in its stage's range and keeps the higher roll. For a Stage 5–6 item (14–22 RP), that raises the average from 18 to about 19.5. Rarity, affixes, and tiers roll as usual.
