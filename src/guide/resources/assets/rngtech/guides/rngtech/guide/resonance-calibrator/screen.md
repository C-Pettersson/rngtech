# Screen

The Resonance Calibrator screen has five tabs:

- **Process**: input, catalyst, stabilizer, and output slots, progress, and energy. Hover the progress bar for time remaining and expected items per minute with your Gear and Mastery applied (chance bonuses such as Super Output are not counted). With the Harmonist's Resonant Streak, the progress tooltip shows your current stability-floor bonus.
- **Gear**: Resonance Coil, Control Board, Stabilizer Matrix, Battery Cell, and pattern slots.
- **Stats**: the machine's current stats, including traits, Gear, and Mastery.
- **Refinement**: refine the placed Calibrator's traits with a catalyst.
- **Mastery**: machine XP, level, and the passive tree.
