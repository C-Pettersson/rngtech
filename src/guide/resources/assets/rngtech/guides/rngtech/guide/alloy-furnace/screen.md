# Screen

The Alloy Furnace screen has six tabs:

- **Process**: input and output slots, progress, energy, live heat, failure strain on recipes that can fail, and the Nitrogen gauge at the right edge. Status squares show the recipe state and whether the heat target is met. Hover for exact values. The progress tooltip also shows time remaining and expected items per minute with your Gear and Mastery applied (chance bonuses such as Super Output are not counted). With an ascendancy, a thin line under the progress bar shows Flux or blend-ledger progress, and Master Blend shows a recipe-disabled status on direct-ingot recipes.
- **Gear**: Heat Core, Alloy Crucible, Battery Cell, and Servo slots.
- **Stats**: the machine's current stats, including traits, Gear, and Mastery.
- **Refinement**: refine the placed Alloy Furnace's traits with a catalyst.
- **Mastery**: machine XP, level, and the passive tree.
- **Orders**: three drive bays for [Job Chips](rngtech:work-orders.md), with each chip's job, progress, and the Activate, Pause, Rush, and Abandon buttons.
