# Gear

The Gear tab has four optional slots:

- **Battery Cell**: adds FE storage on top of the internal buffer. Stage 0–3 cells fit, including the Voltaic Potato Battery Cell.
- **Bio Chamber**: a <ItemLink id="rngtech:bio_chamber" /> adds its rolled power modifiers, and shows as a green culture canister in front of the sight glass. Besides generic FE/t and fuel-duration bonuses, it can roll bonuses for one fuel family, such as potatoes, seeds, Composted Biomass, or algae. These apply only while that family is digesting, and a specific family like saplings uses its own bonus rather than the general plant bonus.
- **Gas Line** (two slots): each takes any Fluid Pump or a Biogas Membrane. A pump starts the [Methane take-off](rngtech:bio-generator/methane-take-off.md). A pump and a membrane show beside the engine while installed.
