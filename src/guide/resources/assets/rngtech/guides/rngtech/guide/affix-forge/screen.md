# Screen

The Affix Forge has a single screen with the target, catalyst, and focus slots, the three upgrade slots, the RP readout, the current-affix list, the outcome preview, and the Reforge button. Hover the RP readout for the exact value, and a lens preview for the matching candidates.

With JEI installed, the catalysts are listed under the Affix Forging category. To find items in the search bar, type `@rngtech affix forge` for the catalysts, lenses, crystals, and upgrades. Type `@rngtech currency` to also list Glyphs for the [Phase Array](rngtech:phase-array.md).
