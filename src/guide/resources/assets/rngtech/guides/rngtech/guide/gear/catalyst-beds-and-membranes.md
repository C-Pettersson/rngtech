# Catalyst Beds and membranes

These single-item parts gate the gas chemistry machines' recipes. The Catalyst Beds tune speed, energy use, and Instant Process. The Fuel Cell Membrane tunes generation, speed, and efficiency. The Biogas Membrane instead sets how much Methane a Bio Generator take-off recovers; it rolls no affixes.

| Part | Used in |
|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:ammonia_catalyst_bed" /> | [Ammonia Synthesizer](rngtech:ammonia-synthesizer.md) (required) |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:reforming_catalyst_bed" /> | [Steam Methane Reformer](rngtech:steam-methane-reformer.md) (required) |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:fuel_cell_membrane" /> | [Fuel Cell](rngtech:ammonia-fuel-cell.md) (required) |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:biogas_membrane" /> | [Bio Generator](rngtech:bio-generator/methane-take-off.md#biogas-membrane) (optional, Gas Line slot) |
