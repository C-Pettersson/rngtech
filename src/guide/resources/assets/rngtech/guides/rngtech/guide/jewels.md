- **Type:** Rolled item
- **Stage:** 3
- **Rarity:** Magic or Rare
- **Sockets:** [Machine Mastery](rngtech:machine-mastery.md) tree, machine bodies

A **jewel** is a small rolled item that you socket into a machine. It carries up to four affixes from one pool that every machine can read. A jewel line works where the machine reads that stat and does nothing where it does not, like a node on the [Machine Mastery](rngtech:machine-mastery.md) tree. Jewels fill the gaps in a build, and a Rare jewel with four good lines is worth chasing.

## Sockets

- [Tree sockets](rngtech:jewels/tree-sockets.md)

### Body sockets

A machine body can have up to four sockets of its own:

- The **Bedazzling** suffix gives 1 socket at Tier 1 to 3, 2 at Tier 4 to 6, and 3 at Tier 7. Every machine with a screen can roll it.
- The Gold [Crusher](rngtech:crusher/stages.md), Gold [Furnace](rngtech:furnace/stages.md), and Gold [Battery Chassis](rngtech:battery-chassis.md) each have one built in.

Body sockets work on machines without Machine Mastery too. Body jewels stay with the body when you break it.

### The Jewels tab

Every machine screen has a **Jewels** tab. The top row is the tree sockets, one slot per allocated socket. The row under it is the body sockets. Put an identified jewel in a slot, or shift-click it from your inventory. Take it out the same way. A jewel never gets used up.

## Refinement

A jewel is a refinement target like any part. The [Affix Forge](rngtech:affix-forge.md) and the [Phase Array](rngtech:phase-array.md) can add, remove, reroll, swap, and corrupt its lines. Jewel lines have one tier, so upgrade catalysts, Resonate, and Exalt have nothing to raise on them.

## Obtaining

### Crafting

Craft a **Jewel Blank** in a crafting grid.

### Finding jewels

Uncut jewels also turn up in village, mineshaft, and ruined portal chests, and more often in igloo basements, bastion treasure rooms, and Nether fortresses. Tier III and higher [Work Orders](rngtech:work-orders.md) can pay one. A found jewel comes Unidentified and was never cut, so it identifies with 2 to 4 RP at random.
