# Stages 5–6: fluids and chemistry

Stages 5 and 6 are about fluids and gases. Gases behave like fluids: they use the same tanks, buckets, and cable connectors. Machine sets now come in Aluminum and Titanium, and older Gear still fits.

1. Craft an <ItemLink id="rngtech:advanced_machine_frame" />. Stage 5 and 6 machine bodies need it.
2. Gasify Coal Dust and water into Syngas in the [Coal Gasifier](rngtech:coal-gasifier.md), and burn it in the [Syngas Combustor](rngtech:syngas-combustor.md). It is the step up from burning coal. Its Carbon Exhaust feeds algae.
3. Build the [Cavitation Generator](rngtech:cavitation-generator.md), which spins a rotor through water for FE.
4. Build the [Silica Gel Dehumidifier](rngtech:silica-gel-dehumidifier.md) for steady water that works underground.
5. Build the [Melter](rngtech:melter.md) and make **Lubricant**, which later Servos and frame parts need. It also makes methane from algae for gas chemistry.
6. Use the [Component Assembler](rngtech:component-assembler.md) to make Battery Cells and other parts in bulk.
7. Reform methane with water in the [Steam Methane Reformer](rngtech:steam-methane-reformer.md) for Hydrogen. Fit the Cavitation Generator with nitrogen Gear to split water into Nitrogen.
8. Combine Nitrogen and Hydrogen in the [Ammonia Synthesizer](rngtech:ammonia-synthesizer.md), and burn the Ammonia in the [Ammonia Fuel Cell](rngtech:ammonia-fuel-cell.md).

Steel and Titanium [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) sets calibrate the components the next stages ask for.
