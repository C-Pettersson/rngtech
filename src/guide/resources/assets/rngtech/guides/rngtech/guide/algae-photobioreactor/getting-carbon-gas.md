# Getting carbon gas

- **Carbon Exhaust**: make it in a [Wooden Dehumidifier](rngtech:wooden-dehumidifier.md) from Coal Dust or Gasification Residue, burn Syngas in a [Syngas Combustor](rngtech:syngas-combustor.md), shift Carbon Monoxide in a [Steam Methane Reformer](rngtech:steam-methane-reformer/water-gas-shift.md), or capture it from a [Bio Generator](rngtech:bio-generator/methane-take-off.md) with a Fluid Pump and a Biogas Membrane. To bootstrap, craft a Carbon Exhaust Bucket from a Water Bucket and one **Coal Dust**. You get Coal Dust by crushing coal in a [Crusher](rngtech:crusher.md).
- **Syngas**: from the [Coal Gasifier](rngtech:coal-gasifier.md).
- **Carbon Monoxide**: a byproduct of the [Steam Methane Reformer](rngtech:steam-methane-reformer.md).
