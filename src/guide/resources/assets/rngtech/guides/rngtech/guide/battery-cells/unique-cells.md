# Unique cells

Three cells are [Uniques](rngtech:uniques.md): found, never crafted, and never refined, though a [Volatile Catalyst](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md#corruption) can corrupt them once.

- The <ItemLink id="rngtech:unique_potato_battery_cell" /> is a rare find in village chests. It stores 10,000 FE with 128 FE/t input, 64 FE/t output, and 6%/min idle loss.
- The <ItemLink id="rngtech:bastion_coin_stack_capacitor" /> comes from bastion treasure chests. It stores 40,000 FE with 512 FE/t input and 128 FE/t output, raises the Burst Transfer and Burst Duration of the [Battery Chassis](rngtech:battery-chassis.md) it sits in, and leaks heavily while idle.
- The <ItemLink id="rngtech:shipwreck_salt_zinc_anode" /> comes from shipwreck supply chests. It is a Stage 1 cell that stores up to 32,400 FE, more than three times an Iron cell. It gives only 32 FE/t and leaks while idle.
