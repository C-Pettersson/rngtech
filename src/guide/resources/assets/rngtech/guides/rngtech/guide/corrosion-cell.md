# Corrosion Cell

<BlockImage id="rngtech:corrosion_cell" scale="6" />

- **Type:** Generator
- **Stages:** 4
- **Power:** FE generator
- **Gear:** <ItemLink id="rngtech:iron_battery_cell" />, <ItemLink id="rngtech:osmium_fluid_pump" />, <ItemLink id="rngtech:aluminum_cathode" />
- **Mastery:** No
- **Tool:** <ItemLink id="minecraft:wooden_pickaxe" />

The **Corrosion Cell** is a Stage 4 generator that makes FE by eating metal, the way an aluminium-air battery does. Each cycle it dissolves one plate, Anode, or piece of Scrap in electrolyte, produces a batch of FE, and leaves behind **Corrosion Residue**. Early on it burns plain Iron and Copper Plates. From Stage 5 you feed it Anodes, which need a matching Cathode installed. You choose how hard to run it: full rated load for the most FE/t, or a lighter load for more FE out of every fuel. See [Power Chains](rngtech:power-chains/corrosion-cell.md) for the machines that make its fuel.

You can watch it work. The fuel hangs from the copper bus bar in a glass electrolyte bath and dissolves from the bottom up. Hydrogen bubbles stream off it, more of them at a higher load, and a faint mist rises from the vent stack at the back. The electrolyte turns cloudy as it fills with dissolved metal and clears with each fresh dose. The installed Cathode lines the back of the bath in its metal's colour, and the blower on top that feeds it air spins with the current. The ammeter on the front shows the current, and the lamp in the other front corner lights while the cell works. Residue settles in the sludge trap at the bottom of the bath as the residue slot fills, and the sight glass on the side shows the electrolyte tank.

Like every RNGTech machine, each Corrosion Cell you craft rolls its own [rarity and affixes](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md).
