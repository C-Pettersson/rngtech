# Molds

| Mold | Makes | Input |
|---|---|---|
| **Plate Mold** | Material plates | 1 ingot |
| **Gear Mold** | Material gears | 3 ingots |
| **Casing Mold** | Material casings, for the metals that use them | 4 ingots |
| **Circuit Mold** | Electric Circuits | 1 circuit blank |
| **Connector Mold** | Energy, Fluid, and Item Connectors for the [Universal Cable](rngtech:universal-cable.md) | 1 coil, casing, or gear; 8 Nullite Coils for the Exotic Energy Connector |

The press stores up to five Molds, so you can keep all of them installed and switch between them in the Gear tab. Selecting another Mold, or swapping the Heat Core, Servo, or a Mold, restarts the current press.
