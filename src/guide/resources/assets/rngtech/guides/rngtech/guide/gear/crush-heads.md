# Crush Heads

A Crush Head sets the **hardness level** your machine can crush, from 1 to 8. The [Crusher](rngtech:crusher.md) can still crush ores above its head's level, but slower, at higher FE cost, and with a chance to jam.

| Crush Head | Stage | Hardness |
|---|---:|---:|
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:flint_crush_head" /> | 0 | 1 |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:iron_crush_head" /> | 1 | 2 |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:copper_crush_head" /> | 2 | 2 |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:bronze_crush_head" /> | 3 | 3 |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:steel_crush_head" /> | 4 | 4 |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:aluminum_crush_head" /> | 5 | 5 |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:titanium_crush_head" /> | 6 | 6 |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:tungstensteel_crush_head" /> | 7 | 7 |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:exotic_crush_head" /> | 8 | 8 |

The Flint Crush Head is cheap starter tooling with slower processing.

**Used in:** [Crusher](rngtech:crusher.md) (required, up to the chassis stage), [Melter](rngtech:melter.md) (required, any stage, sets the processing level for melts), and the <ItemLink id="rngtech:miners_companion" /> (required).
