# Crafting

<RecipesFor id="rngtech:requisition" />

- **Calibrated Logic Component** must be a Logic calibration, Stage 2+, stability 80+.
- Requires the Steel material to be enabled.
- **Calibrated Logic Component** must be a Logic calibration, Stage 2+, stability 65+.

Each recipe needs a Calibrated Logic Component from the [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md):

- The Dispatch Terminal needs a component with 80 or more stability. Only the Stage 4 Calibrator's resonant recipe reaches that.
- The Requisition needs a component with 65 or more stability. One craft makes two Requisitions.

Job Chips and Completed Job Chips have no recipe. Chips come from a Dispatch Terminal, and some quests give them out.
