# Crafting rotors

Every Cavitation Rotor needs a **Cavitation Rotor Hub**: a Steel Casing calibrated in the [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) with the Kinetic pattern. Better rotors need a better-calibrated hub:

| Rotor | Minimum hub stability |
|---|---|
| Steel | 20 |
| Titanium, Nitrogen Extraction | 35 |
| Tungstensteel, Aethergold | 50 |
| Nullite | 65 |

A hub's stability also skews the rotor's resonance upward, so a precise hub is worth keeping for the rotor you care about most. A hub that rolls too low for what you need recycles back into its Steel Casing in a Stage 5 [Component Recycler](rngtech:component-recycler.md).
