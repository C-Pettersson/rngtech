# Fuel Boxes

The Fuel Box sets a burner's fuel slots, which fuel forms it burns, and fuel behaviors such as the Fuel Governor. A burner can use a [Fuel Tank or Gas Jet](rngtech:fuel-burner/fluid-fuels.md) in its place to burn fluid.

| Fuel Box | Stage | Fuel slots | Fuel forms |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:iron_fuel_box" /> | 1 | 1 | Items |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:copper_fuel_box" /> | 2 | 1 | Items |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:bronze_fuel_box" /> | 3 | 2 | Items |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:steel_fuel_box" /> | 4 | 2 | Items and compact fuel blocks |

**Used in:** [Fuel Burner](rngtech:fuel-burner.md) (required, up to the burner's stage). That page explains each box's behaviors.
