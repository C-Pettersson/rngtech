# Identification

A freshly crafted item comes out **Unidentified**, for example "Unidentified Iron Furnace Chassis". Its rarity, affixes, and RP are hidden, and the crafting preview never shows them, so you cannot reroll a recipe by peeking at the result.

To identify an item, put it **alone** in a crafting grid and take the result. The preview still says Unidentified; the traits appear when you take the item out. Other ways to identify:

- **Placing** an unidentified machine rolls its traits as it goes down.
- **Installing** an unidentified part rolls its traits as it goes into its slot. This applies to [Gear](rngtech:gear.md) in a machine or companion, to Battery Cells in a [Battery Chassis](rngtech:battery-chassis.md) or a socket, and to the head, rod, and cell you build into a tool at the [Tool Bench](rngtech:tool-bench.md).
- Battery Cells made in a [Component Assembler](rngtech:component-assembler.md) come out already identified.

An item you put in a machine to process stays unidentified. For example, a part in a [Component Recycler](rngtech:component-recycler.md) input slot does not roll.

You cannot refine an unidentified item. Identify it first.
