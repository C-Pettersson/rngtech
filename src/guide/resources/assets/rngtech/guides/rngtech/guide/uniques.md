# Uniques

**Uniques** are [Gear](rngtech:gear.md) and [Battery Cells](rngtech:battery-cells.md) you find, never craft. Each has an orange-brown name, the unique colour from Path of Exile, a **signature** that no normal part of its type has, and a **drawback** you can see on the item. Some also carry an **ascendancy bonus** that works only on a machine that chose that ascendancy (see [Machine Mastery](rngtech:machine-mastery.md#ascendancies)).

A Unique is a sidegrade, not a higher stage. It lets a machine do something different, and it costs you something for it.

Every copy of a Unique has the same stat lines, but each copy rolls its own values inside the ranges listed below. You can find a well-rolled copy or a poorly rolled one.

## The Uniques

Ranges are listed worst roll to best. Flat lines with whole-number ranges, such as Jam Recovery, roll whole numbers.

### Trial Vault Tempered Pawl

A Disassembly Head for the [Component Recycler](rngtech:component-recycler.md) that takes apart parts one stage above its own. Its other base stats are a Bronze Disassembly Head's. It comes from normal trial vaults, not ominous ones.

- **Signature:** +1 processing level, so a Bronze Recycler with the Pawl runs Stage 4 recipes.
- **Drawbacks:** (40–20)% increased Energy Use, and (25–10)% less Processing Speed.

- [Trial Vault Escapement](rngtech:uniques/trial-vault-escapement.md)
- [Witch-Bottle Reflux Pump](rngtech:uniques/witch-bottle-reflux-pump.md)
- [Ancient Echo Control Board](rngtech:uniques/ancient-echo-control-board.md)

### Bastion Coin-Stack Capacitor

A burst Battery Cell. It holds 40,000 FE, takes 512 FE/t, and gives a steady 128 FE/t, half an Invar cell's output.

- **Signature:** (75–125)% increased Burst Transfer and (75–125)% increased Burst Duration for the [Battery Chassis](rngtech:battery-chassis.md) it is in. Only a chassis that can burst uses them.
- **Drawback:** (150–100)% increased Idle Loss.

### Shipwreck Salt-Zinc Anode

A Stage 1 Battery Cell that stores far more than an Iron cell. Its base holds 24,000 FE, takes 128 FE/t, and gives 32 FE/t, half an Iron cell's output. Its efficiency is 90%, and its base idle loss is 0.3%/min.

- **Signature:** (15–35)% increased Energy Capacity, so it holds 27,600 to 32,400 FE.
- **Drawback:** (100–50)% increased Idle Loss.

### Voltaic Potato Battery Cell

A potato wired to hold far more than it should. It stores 10,000 FE with 128 FE/t input, 64 FE/t output, and 6%/min idle loss. It has no ranges to roll. See [Battery Cells](rngtech:battery-cells/unique-cells.md).
