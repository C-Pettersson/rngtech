# Tree sockets

The [Machine Mastery](rngtech:machine-mastery/node-types.md) tree has nine **Jewel Sockets**. A socket grants nothing itself. Each socket you allocate opens one jewel slot on the machine's **Jewels** tab. Hover a socket on the Mastery tab to see the jewel it holds and which of its lines this machine reads.

Refunding a socket, or clearing the tree, leaves the jewel in its slot. The jewel does nothing until a socket is allocated again. A Soul Transfer carries the tree's jewels with the Mastery.
