# Annealing

Two ascendancy notables let the press anneal what it makes under Hydrogen: Die Keeper's **Bright Anneal**, after Purge Line, and Drop Forge's **Hydrogen Quench**, after Quench Line. With either one, the press's [atmosphere tank](rngtech:heat/nitrogen-atmosphere.md) also takes **Hydrogen Bucket** Hydrogen. The tank holds one gas at a time, so it takes Hydrogen only while it holds no Nitrogen; pump the tank out with a [Universal Cable](rngtech:universal-cable.md) to switch gases. Without either notable it refuses Hydrogen.

- **Bright Anneal**: Basic Electric Circuits pressed while the tank holds Hydrogen come out Annealed.
- **Hydrogen Quench**: plates pressed while the tank holds Hydrogen come out Annealed.

Each Annealed item costs 250 mB of Hydrogen. Other outputs press normally and use none. A Drop Forge batch presses only as many plates as the tank can pay for, so a batch of 6 plates needs 1,500 mB. Hover the gauge on an annealing press to see what it anneals and the cost.

Annealed items show an aqua **Annealed** line in their tooltip. They do not stack with plain copies, and every recipe accepts them like plain ones. What annealing gives you:

- An Annealed Basic Electric Circuit calibrates with [lucky stability](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md#annealed-circuits) in the Resonance Calibrator.
- Gear crafted with only Annealed plates rolls [lucky Refinement Potential](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes/annealed-items.md).
