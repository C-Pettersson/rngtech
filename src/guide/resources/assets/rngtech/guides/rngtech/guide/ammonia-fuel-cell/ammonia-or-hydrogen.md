# Ammonia or Hydrogen

Hydrogen is worth more as Ammonia. Turned into Ammonia in the [Ammonia Synthesizer](rngtech:ammonia-synthesizer.md) and burned here, each mB of Hydrogen makes about 136 FE after the synthesis cost. Burned directly, it makes 60 FE. Burning Hydrogen skips the Cavitation Generator and the Synthesizer, so it suits a small build or a Reformer with Hydrogen to spare. Once your Ammonia chain runs, send the Hydrogen there instead.
