# Refinement

The [Affix Forge](rngtech:affix-forge.md) changes the affixes on loose machine items, Gear parts, and Battery Cells. Placed machines refine from their own Refinement tab, and tool parts refine in the [Tool Bench](rngtech:tool-bench.md#refining-tool-parts). Every operation spends Refinement Potential.

- The base forge adds an affix with an <ItemLink id="rngtech:affix_injector" />, upgrades a random affix with an <ItemLink id="rngtech:affix_modifier" />, and removes a random affix with a <ItemLink id="rngtech:nullifier_coil" />.
- Forge upgrades unlock lenses that steer which affix you get, crystals that change an operation's cost or side effects, and catalysts that raise a Magic item to Rare.

Spend the budget deliberately. A Magic part with two good affixes is a strong candidate to raise to Rare. See [Rarity and Affixes](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes.md) for how rolls and costs work.
