# Stages

| Stage | Chassis | Lanes | Notes |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:iron_resonance_calibrator_chassis" /> | 1 | First Calibrator. Reaches the Stage 2 parts needed for Steel. |
| 2 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:copper_resonance_calibrator_chassis" /> | 1 | Optional faster sidegrade with weaker stability. |
| 4 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:steel_resonance_calibrator_chassis" /> | 1 | Slower but more stable. |
| 4 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:lead_resonance_calibrator_chassis" /> | 2 | Two-lane bulk sidegrade with lower precision. |
| 6 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:titanium_resonance_calibrator_chassis" /> | 1 | Precision chassis with better speed, stability, and Refinement Potential. |
| 7 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:tungstensteel_resonance_calibrator_chassis" /> | 3 | Three-lane late-game bulk branch. |
| 7 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:nullite_resonance_calibrator_chassis" /> | 1 | Late-game precision branch with the best quality and Refinement Potential. |

A multi-lane chassis calibrates that many copies of the input per cycle, each paying its own FE. A cycle runs only as many copies as the input, catalyst, and stabilizer stacks allow. All copies from one cycle share the same stability and Refinement Potential roll.
