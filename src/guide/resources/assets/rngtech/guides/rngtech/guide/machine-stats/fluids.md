# Fluids

| Stat | What it does | Used by | Better |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fluid Capacity | Size of the machine's fluid tank. Fluid Pumps can roll it for the machine they sit in. | [Melter](rngtech:melter.md), [Corrosion Cell](rngtech:corrosion-cell.md), [Compressor Tanks](rngtech:compressor-tank.md), the Forestry Companion's water tank. | Higher |
| Fluid Transfer | How fast fluid moves in or out, in mB/t. Usually set by an installed Fluid Pump; on Compressor Tanks it is the compression rate before Servos. | [Component Assembler](rngtech:component-assembler.md), [Corrosion Cell](rngtech:corrosion-cell.md), [Melter](rngtech:melter.md), [Cavitation Generator](rngtech:cavitation-generator.md), [Compressor Tanks](rngtech:compressor-tank.md). | Higher |
| Compression Ratio | How much fluid a Steel or higher Compressor Tank packs into its compressed store. Set by the tank's material. | [Compressor Tanks](rngtech:compressor-tank.md). | Higher |
