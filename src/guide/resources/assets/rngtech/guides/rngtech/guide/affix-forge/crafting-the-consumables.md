The Volatile Catalyst asks you to make three machines go wrong on purpose:

- a Stage 5 Calibrated Kinetic Component with stability between 20% and 35%. A well-built [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md) overshoots that band, so mistune it: a weaker Control Board, no Stabilizer Matrix, or no Battery Cell;
- two Malformed Ingots of a Stage 5 or higher material, from failed [Furnace](rngtech:furnace.md), [Metal Press](rngtech:metal-press.md), or [Alloy Furnace](rngtech:alloy-furnace.md) cycles;
- two <ItemLink id="rngtech:jam_debris" /> from [Crusher](rngtech:crusher/hardness.md#jam-debris) jams.

<RecipesFor id="rngtech:volatile_catalyst" />

- **Calibrated Kinetic Component** must be a Kinetic calibration, Stage 5+, stability 20-35.
- Requires the Sparksteel material to be enabled.

The Mirror Catalyst has no recipe. Only a [Work Order](rngtech:work-orders/rewards.md) of Tier IV or V can pay it.
