# Automation

Connectors work on any side: every face inserts fuel and exports FE to adjacent receivers, and no face accepts FE. With a Fluid Pump installed, every face also gives Methane and Carbon Exhaust, and no face accepts fluid. The **automation port** on the back, the plant's grid-connection cabinet with a copper-pinned socket in its door and a copper conduit from the engine, marks the spot where a [Universal Cable](rngtech:universal-cable.md) connector seats flush against the machine.

Automation cannot extract items, and the Gear slots are installed by hand.
