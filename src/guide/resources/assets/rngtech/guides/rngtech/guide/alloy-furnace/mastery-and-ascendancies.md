# Mastery and Ascendancies

Completed alloy crafts earn Alloy Furnace Mastery XP, and you spend the points on the shared [Machine Mastery](rngtech:machine-mastery.md) tree, starting from the same point as the Furnace. Failures, stalled heat, blocked output, and power-starved ticks earn nothing. Alloy Furnaces choose between the **Metallurgist** and **Blendwright** [ascendancies](rngtech:machine-mastery.md#ascendancies).

The heat keystones cap Max Temperature, and every alloy needs at least 900 heat. **Flash Annealing** caps it at 1,000 heat, which leaves four recipes in the [recipe table](rngtech:alloy-furnace.md), and gives 100% more Processing Speed with 50% more Energy Usage. **Low Heat Specialist** caps it at 800 heat, which is below every alloy, and its savings only work on the Furnace, so an Alloy Furnace with it allocated cannot craft anything.

**Quench Protocol** halves the temperature failure strain the Alloy Furnace takes, for 25% less Heat Transfer.

Metallurgist Flux only saves a unit of an ingredient the recipe needs two or more of, so every input is still used. On a tie, it saves the ingredient listed first in the recipe.
