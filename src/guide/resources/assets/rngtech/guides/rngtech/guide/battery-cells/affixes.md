# Affixes

Cells roll affixes like any other Gear. Capacity affixes come in two kinds: flat (+FE) and percentage (increased capacity). Flat bonuses apply first, so a Copper Cell with +4,000 FE and 25% increased capacity holds (12,000 + 4,000) × 1.25 = 20,000 FE. Other affixes improve transfer rates or reduce leakage. Cells do not roll Efficiency. An older cell can still show an Efficiency affix. That affix has no effect, and the Affix Forge can remove it. A [Battery Chassis](rngtech:battery-chassis.md) has its own Efficiency. You can refine standard cells in the [Affix Forge](rngtech:affix-forge.md).
