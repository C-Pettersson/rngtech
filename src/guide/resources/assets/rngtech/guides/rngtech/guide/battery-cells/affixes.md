# Affixes

Cells roll affixes like any other Gear. Capacity affixes come in two kinds: flat (+FE) and percentage (increased capacity). Flat bonuses apply first, so a Copper Cell with +4,000 FE and 25% increased capacity holds (12,000 + 4,000) × 1.25 = 20,000 FE. Other affixes improve transfer rates or reduce leakage. Cells can also roll Efficiency, and the Left Shift tooltip shows an Efficiency value, but it has no effect on how a cell charges or discharges. You can refine standard cells in the [Affix Forge](rngtech:affix-forge.md).
