# Alloys

Alloys have no ore. You make them in the [Alloy Furnace](rngtech:alloy-furnace.md), and the Alloy Furnace chassis and its Crucible must both reach the recipe's stage.

| Alloy | Stage | Made from | How |
|---|---:|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:bronze_ingot" /> | 3 | Copper and tin | Craft **Bronze Blend** from Copper, Tin, and Coal Dust and smelt it in a [Furnace](rngtech:furnace.md), or alloy Copper and Tin Ingots with Charcoal. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:steel_ingot" /> | 4 | Iron and carbon | Make **Steel Blend** in the Alloy Furnace and smelt it, or alloy Iron Ingots with Coal, or with Coal Dust and Charcoal. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:invar_ingot" /> | 4 | Iron and nickel | Alloy Furnace. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:sparksteel_ingot" /> | 5 | Gold, silver, and redstone | Alloy Furnace, Steel stage or higher. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:arclite_ingot" /> | 6 | Copper, silver, and redstone | Alloy Furnace, Steel stage or higher. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:tungstensteel_ingot" /> | 7 | Tungsten, steel, and Coal Dust | Titanium Alloy Furnace. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:nullite_ingot" /> | 7 | Lead, platinum, and Ender Pearls | Titanium Alloy Furnace. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:aethergold_ingot" /> | 7 | Tin, silver, and Glowstone Dust | Titanium Alloy Furnace. |

See the [Alloy Furnace](rngtech:alloy-furnace.md) recipe table for exact amounts and temperatures.
