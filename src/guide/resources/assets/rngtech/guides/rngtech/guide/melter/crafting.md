# Crafting

The Melter's recipe needs a calibrated conductive component of Stage 5 or higher with at least 70 stability. You make one by calibrating a Sparksteel Coil in the [Resonance Calibrator](rngtech:resonance-calibrator.md).

<RecipesFor id="rngtech:melter" />

- **Calibrated Conductive Component** must be a Conductive calibration, Stage 5+, stability 70+.
- Requires the Titanium material to be enabled.
