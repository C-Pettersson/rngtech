# Mastery and Ascendancies

Every placed machine earns its own Mastery XP from successful work and spends points in the passive tree on its Mastery tab. Mastery stays with the machine when you break and move it, so level the machines you plan to keep.

A machine with a Stage 4 or higher chassis can spend an <ItemLink id="rngtech:ascendancy_seal_1" /> from your inventory to choose one of its family's ascendancies. Seals are crafted from Stage 5 materials, and Seals II and III from Stage 7 and 8 materials. See [Ascendancies](rngtech:machine-mastery.md#ascendancies).
