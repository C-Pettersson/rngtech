# Calibration families

There are six patterns, one per family: **Structural Calibration Pattern**, **Kinetic Calibration Pattern**, **Thermal Calibration Pattern**, **Conductive Calibration Pattern**, **Storage Calibration Pattern**, and **Logic Calibration Pattern**. The Logic family also makes the **Calibrated Diamond Crystal**, a Stage 6 crystal needed for the Stage 7 chassis and late Stabilizer Matrices. The Kinetic family also makes the **Cavitation Rotor Hub** from a Steel Casing, a Stage 5 hub every [Cavitation Rotor](rngtech:cavitation-generator/crafting-rotors.md) needs. Its stability, from 20% to 80%, decides which rotors it can build and pushes the rotor's resonance higher.

The Stage 5 Kinetic recipe from a Sparksteel Gear rolls a low, wide stability range on purpose. The [Volatile Catalyst](rngtech:affix-forge/crafting-the-consumables.md) needs one between 20% and 35%, which a well-built Calibrator overshoots.

Several families have a "resonant" recipe that adds a Matrix and a Stabilization Catalyst. It needs Stage 4 reach and gives a higher and tighter stability range, for recipes that demand well-calibrated parts.
