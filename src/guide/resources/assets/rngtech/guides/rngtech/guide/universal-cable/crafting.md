One craft makes 12 Cables or 2 Universal Connectors. The <ItemLink id="rngtech:crude_energy_connector" /> is the only connector module you can craft by hand.

The network connectors have recipes only when their mod is installed:

- <ItemLink id="rngtech:ae2_network_connector" />: a Universal Connector, two Basic Energy Connectors, an AE2 Engineering Processor, Fluix Glass Cables, and redstone.
- <ItemLink id="rngtech:refined_storage_network_connector" />: a Universal Connector, four Basic Item Connectors, Refined Storage Cables, and a Construction Core and Destruction Core.
