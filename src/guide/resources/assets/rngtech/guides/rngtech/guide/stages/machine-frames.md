# Machine frames

Most staged machine bodies are built around a frame for their stage band:

| Frame | Used by |
|---|---|
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:machine_frame" /> | Stage 0–3 bodies, and the Steel Metal Press. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:reinforced_machine_frame" /> | Most Stage 4 Steel and Lead bodies. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:advanced_machine_frame" /> | Stage 5–6 bodies. |
| <ItemLink id="rngtech:exotic_machine_frame" /> | Stage 7–8 bodies. |

Frames are plain crafting ingredients: they have no stats or affixes, and a frame's tier does not change the finished machine's stats. Each frame is crafted from the one before it. A Reinforced Machine Frame adds 12 Steel Ingots, pressed into four Steel Plates and two Steel Casings in six [Metal Press](rngtech:metal-press.md) runs. The Exotic frame also needs a <ItemLink id="rngtech:lubricated_frame_coupling" />, assembled from Tungstensteel Casing and Lubricant in the [Component Assembler](rngtech:component-assembler.md).
