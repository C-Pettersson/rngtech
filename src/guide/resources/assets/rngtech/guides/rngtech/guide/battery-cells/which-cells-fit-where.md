# Which cells fit where

The [Phase Cradle](rngtech:phase-array.md#gear) has nine sockets that take any cell. While the line charges, each cell drains into the charge at its own output rate, like a Dormant Machine's sockets.

Most slots take any cell. These machines limit the stage:

| Machine | Cell slot |
|---|---|
| [Solid Fuel Burner](rngtech:solid-fuel-burner.md) | Required. Up to the burner's stage: Stage 1 on the Crude burner, up to Stage 4 on the Steel burner. |
| [Bio Generator](rngtech:bio-generator.md) | Optional, Stage 0–3. |
| [Coal Gasifier](rngtech:coal-gasifier.md), [Syngas Combustor](rngtech:syngas-combustor.md) | Optional, Stage 5 or higher. |
| [Steam Methane Reformer](rngtech:steam-methane-reformer.md) | Optional, Stage 6 or higher. |
| [Fuel Cell](rngtech:ammonia-fuel-cell.md) | Optional, Stage 6 or higher. |
| [Compressor Tank](rngtech:compressor-tank.md) (Steel and higher) | Required, up to the tank's stage. |

Modular tools and the <ItemLink id="rngtech:miners_companion" /> also run on an installed Battery Cell. The [Tool Bench](rngtech:tool-bench.md) installs cells in modular tools and charges both; the Miner's Companion takes its cell in its own Gear tab.
