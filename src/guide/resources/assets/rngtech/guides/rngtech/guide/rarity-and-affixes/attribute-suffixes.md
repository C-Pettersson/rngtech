# Attribute suffixes

Machines with [Machine Mastery](rngtech:machine-mastery.md) and the Gear they take can roll **+Control** (of Command), **+Drive** (of Impetus), and **+Reserve** (of Reserves). Each is a flat number of points, from 3–5 at Tier 1 to 36–45 at Tier 7. Unlike other part affixes, these points count for the whole machine, and they feed the attribute bonuses and attribute scaling exactly like points from the tree. Each attribute is its own group, so one item can roll both +Control and +Drive but never two +Drive.

They roll on the Crusher, Furnace, Alloy Furnace, Metal Press, Melter, Resonance Calibrator, and Forestry Companion, and on Crush Heads, Heat Cores, Servos, Fluid Pumps, Alloy Crucibles, Resonance Coils, Control Boards, and Stabilizer Matrices. A part that sits in a machine without Mastery, such as a Heat Core in a Solid Fuel Burner, gets nothing from them.
