# Crafting

Each chassis has its own recipe, and none of them use up an older Calibrator, so a well-rolled early chassis stays useful. Steel and higher chassis need calibrated components of a given family, stage, and stability, shown in the Notes column.

- **Calibrated Structural Component** must be a Structural calibration, Stage 1+, stability 55+.
- **Calibrated Logic Component** must be a Logic calibration, Stage 2+, stability 55+.
- Requires the Steel material to be enabled.
- **Calibrated Structural Component** must be a Structural calibration, Stage 2+, stability 70+.
- **Calibrated Conductive Component** must be a Conductive calibration, Stage 2+, stability 70+.
- Requires the Titanium material to be enabled.
- **Calibrated Structural Component** must be a Structural calibration, Stage 1+, stability 45+.
- **Calibrated Storage Component** must be a Storage calibration, Stage 2+, stability 45+.
- Requires the Lead material to be enabled.
- **Calibrated Structural Component** must be a Structural calibration, Stage 3+, stability 85+.
- **Calibrated Diamond Crystal** must be a Logic calibration, Stage 6+, stability 85+.
- Requires the Tungstensteel material to be enabled.
- **Calibrated Conductive Component** must be a Conductive calibration, Stage 2+, stability 85+.
- Requires the Nullite material to be enabled.
