# Stages

| Stage | Burner | Notes |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:crude_solid_fuel_burner" /> | Accepts Stage 1 Gear. Lowest stability. |
| 2 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:copper_solid_fuel_burner" /> | Accepts Gear up to Stage 2. |
| 3 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:alloy_solid_fuel_burner" /> | Accepts Gear up to Stage 3. Has Fuel Reserve. |
| 4 | <ItemLink id="rngtech:steel_solid_fuel_burner" /> | Accepts Gear up to Stage 4. Highest stability. |

Every burner also accepts lower-stage Gear, so you can mix parts to trade speed against fuel efficiency. For more FE/t than a Steel burner gives, move on to the Stage 5 [Syngas Combustor](rngtech:syngas-combustor.md).
