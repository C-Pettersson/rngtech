# Rolls and refinement

Each crafted part rolls its own rarity and affixes, and you can refine it in the [Affix Forge](rngtech:affix-forge.md). Parts do not stack. An unidentified part rolls when you install it, so you do not have to [identify](rngtech:rarity-and-affixes/identification.md) it first. A part's affixes are **local**: they scale that part's own stats, and the result is then added to the machine. Holding Shift on a tooltip marks these rolls "(Local)". Rolls without the mark, such as a Fluid Pump's Fluid Capacity, apply to the machine as they are.

Higher-stage parts are crafted from fresh materials, not from the previous part. You can keep a well-rolled part in service and craft the next stage separately. The <ItemLink id="rngtech:nullite_servo" /> is the one part that upgrades from an older part, the <ItemLink id="rngtech:titanium_servo" />. It rolls fresh traits: the Titanium Servo's affixes and RP are lost.

Many parts you no longer need can be broken down for materials in a [Component Recycler](rngtech:component-recycler.md).
