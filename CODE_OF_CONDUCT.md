# Code of Conduct

Treat players, contributors, and maintainers with respect. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Explain disagreements with examples and accept that others may have different needs or experience.
- Welcome people regardless of their background, identity, disability, or level of experience.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, post sexual content directed at others, or disclose someone's private information without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam or repeated demands for a response. Respect moderation decisions.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed. They will consider the behavior's impact, severity, and whether it is repeated. Threats, harassment, and disclosure of private information may lead to an immediate ban.

Use [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) to report abusive content or private harassment. Do not repeat private information in a public issue. Report security vulnerabilities through the separate channel in [Security](SECURITY.md).
